Euphonia: tool access

Euphonia reads your hub freely and writes only to her own notes. Anything else needs a grant here, per server. Read allows look-up style tools (get, list, search, read, fetch, query, describe, find, view, check, analyze, preview). Write also allows everything else, including any tool the app does not recognise. She cannot change this herself, and nothing she reads or says can. Even with write access she shows you the exact text and destination in the chat and waits for your reply before each write.

Euphonia: name and voice

All save as soon as you change them and are read back from disk to confirm. Your name and pronouns are how Euphonia addresses you in chat (never your login); leave the name empty for "the owner". The pack plays when Euphonia finishes a reply, starting with the next one. Renaming her in the Pets tab does the same.

Whose voice is heard

With a Pixoo connected, the voice follows what it shows. Without one, it follows the main window's view (the pet view is the lead, Euphonia); her reply cue also plays while her chat window is focused. Everyone else's cue is dropped, not queued. This filters sounds Peon Pet plays itself (Euphonia's replies). Sounds from the peon-ping hook run outside this app: the current focus is published to voice-focus.json in the app's data folder for it to consult.

Kinds of agent

A pet can serve as many kinds of agent. Each kind belongs to a Firm role and has a viewpoint (personality), traits that decide when it fits, and permissions it may only narrow from its role. Sub-agents are matched automatically: the type whose traits fit the agent's role, project, title and folder best wins, and ties go to the type used least so duplicates get variety. Pin a type on one agent from the Projects page. The Firm's own choice always wins.

Monitor: recent activity

Peon Pet watches what agents wearing a kind actually do. A tool call outside the kind's permissions is logged as a violation (with who should have done it); a finished task is a credit. Your own sessions are never judged. The Firm will eventually supply these events itself.
